### 1. WanderBench: Can World Models Explore Dynamic Worlds from Video?

**中文标题**: WanderBench: Can World Models Explore Dynamic Worlds from Video?

**作者**: Yuhang Chen, Jiheng Li, Jiale Wang

**中文摘要**: Video world models conditioned on an input video make it possible to explore real, recorded dynamic scenes, enabling re-shooting of footage, free-viewpoint replay, and real-to-sim environments for embodied agents. Such uses require the generated rollout to stay faithful to the recorded world, yet existing benchmarks start from a single image or a text prompt, and video-conditioned generation is evaluated mainly on perceptual quality and camera following. These evaluations do not directly assess whether exploration preserves subject identity, event progression, and scene consistency upon revisiting a location. We introduce WanderBench, a benchmark for exploring dynamic worlds from video. It comprises 745 single-shot source videos of real and AI-generated content in three difficulty tiers (3-25 s), paired with camera routes given as keyboard actions, and evaluates six dimensions with 24 human-validated sub-metrics. Benchmarking eight models, we find that as clips grow longer, models keep the static scene and visual quality but lose camera control and the source dynamics, and that none keeps revisited places consistent. We further propose WanderLand, a training-free framework that conditions each frame of a frozen model on the same-instant source and, on revisits, on its own earlier static content. WanderLand improves five models on every dimension, by up to 8.3 points in source-dynamics fidelity.

### 2. SciMARS: Multi-Agent Recursive Self-Improvement for Closed-Loop Scientific Discovery

**中文标题**: SciMARS: Multi-Agent Recursive Self-Improvement for Closed-Loop Scientific Discovery

**作者**: Weimin Wu, Han Liu

**中文摘要**: We propose SciMARS, a framework for recursive task-local adaptation of multi-agent harnesses in closed-loop scientific discovery. Multi-agent systems coordinate analysis, experimental design, and verification as scientific evidence evolves. However, two problems limit their effectiveness: (i) without shared memory, agents lose observations and repeat work; (ii) fixed role procedures, handoffs, and memory policies perpetuate mistakes despite retained evidence. We address these problems with a shared evidence graph and separate component editors. SciMARS adapts roles and coordination; SciMARS + Memory adds a third editor for how agents record, retrieve, and receive evidence. Candidate probes guide composition and selection of the next harness. We evaluate scientific discovery through experimental-data replay and a digital twin. SciMARS + Memory resolves 12/18 GPT-6-Astra and 2/18 GLM-5.3 attempts on Autonomous Science Bench, compared with 8/18 and 1/18 for the base multi-agent team. On ScienceAgentBench and AppWorld, it solves 14/38 and 11/20 tasks, compared with 11/38 and 5/20 for the base team. These results support combining shared memory with harness adaptation across scientific and interactive tasks.

### 3. A Strict Upper Bound for Three-Point Weighted Selection in Planar Vector-Valued Regression

**中文标题**: A Strict Upper Bound for Three-Point Weighted Selection in Planar Vector-Valued Regression

**作者**: Sergey Volkov

**中文摘要**: We prove $F_{\mathrm{weighted}}(2,2,3)<11/6$ for minimum-Frobenius-norm least-squares regression with two input and two output coordinates, improving the upper bound $15/8$. More precisely, a universal $\varepsilon_0>0$ satisfies $\mathcal T_3\le(5/6-\varepsilon_0)E$ for every finite residual frame. The proof combines an exact scalar certificate, rigidity of equality in planar selection, and a compactness argument that controls disappearing weights. Together with the known lower bound, this gives $13/8\le F_{\mathrm{weighted}}(2,2,3)<11/6$. No numerical value of $\varepsilon_0$ is claimed.

### 4. Teamknitt: Configuring, Observing and Intervening in LLM-Agent Teams through the Lens of Dynamical-Systems and Human-Team Theory

**中文标题**: Teamknitt: Configuring, Observing and Intervening in LLM-Agent Teams through the Lens of Dynamical-Systems and Human-Team Theory

**作者**: Goran Mitevski

**中文摘要**: Most frameworks for teams of large language model (LLM) agents fix the team's shape inside the software: who talks to whom, how much is passed along, and what a human can do once a run has started. Human-team research has long asked those questions: where team phenomena live (in members or in their interaction), how team-level properties arise, and what separates what a team does from how a team is. I propose using that theory as the organizing lens for configuring, observing and intervening in LLM-agent teams, without assuming its findings transfer.   I introduce teamknitt, a framework and open reference implementation in which a user configures a team of 1-16 AI agents for task completion, solution generation, achieving a goal or a research mission; selects one of fourteen deterministic routing topologies or a custom directed graph; chooses a sharing profile that limits what may travel along permitted routes; and binds sources, representations, work dependencies, handoffs and an expertise directory. The recorded process can be observed through non-system views (member reports and behavior) and system views (distributed problem-solving, differentiated transactive memory, interactive coordination, team dynamics). Interventions are explicit and human-led: reviewed reconfiguration between runs, planned disturbances, and private feedback, pause, resume or stop applied at dependency-wave boundaries, with "queued", "applied", "assembled", "understood" and "improved" kept as separate claims. Emergent states such as trust and shared understanding are declared unmeasured.   This is an idea and system-description paper with falsifiable hypotheses on topology, information flow and intervention timing, including whether the fractal scaling of communication output between members, and between members on different models, relates to task and team performance. No comparative result is reported.

### 5. Learning inexact alternating minimization for convex problems with quadratic coupling

**中文标题**: Learning inexact alternating minimization for convex problems with quadratic coupling

**作者**: Paul Häusner, Jevgenija Rudzusika, Jens Sjölund

**中文摘要**: Alternating minimization is a standard tool for optimization problems with two coupled variable blocks. Its cost is dominated by the inner subproblem solvers, which rarely have a closed-form solution. In this paper, we propose to learn an inexact alternating scheme in which neural networks approximate the subproblem solution at each iteration. For convex problems with quadratic couplings between the variable blocks that satisfy a quadratic growth condition, we derive a worst-case bound on the optimality gap that exposes the per-iteration error that each network is trained to minimize in a greedy fashion. Combining the bound with a PAC-Bayes argument, we obtain a fixed-horizon, high-probability bound on the expected optimality gap over the distribution of problem instances. We apply the learned scheme to robust matrix completion and low-dose CT reconstruction with dictionary regularization where we achieve a 5x-13x speedup over the inexact first-order baseline at matched accuracy. A brief end-to-end fine-tuning stage extends this up to 30x and outperforms other learned schemes. The code is available at https://github.com/paulhausner/learned-alternating-minimization.
