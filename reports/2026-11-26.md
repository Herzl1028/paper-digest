### 1. Spectrum Adaptation of AI-Native Autoencoder Communications with Transfer Learning

**中文标题**: Spectrum Adaptation of AI-Native Autoencoder Communications with Transfer Learning

**作者**: Yalin E. Sagduyu, Tugba Erpek

**中文摘要**: End-to-end physical-layer learning replaces separate modulation, coding, and detection operations with an autoencoder (AE) that consists of a jointly trained encoder-decoder pair at the transmitter and the receiver. This paper studies AI-native communications with AEs and quantifies the sensitivity to training-test mismatch in channel models. Models trained only on an AWGN channel achieve low bit error rates (BERs) under matched conditions but degrade severely on Rayleigh fading with practical front-end impairments. To reduce the time and energy required to adapt to new channel conditions, we propose a low-latency transfer-learning procedure that starts from an AWGN-pretrained system and fine-tunes only a small subset of transmitter and receiver layers while freezing the remainder. We further formulate layer selection as a budget-constrained optimization problem that identifies the most informative parameters to update during adaptation. This preserves the learned signal geometry and the deep decision structure while adapting lightweight front ends to fading- and impairment-induced mixing. For AE communications, we show that partial updates remove the mismatch-induced error floor and approach the BER of fully retrained models. We provide a layer-wise study and quantify resource-performance trade-offs, showing that modest adaptation substantially reduces BER while cutting training time, multiply-accumulate (MAC) operations, energy consumption, and the fraction of updated parameters for resource-efficient implementation.

### 2. Tokenisation is not in APX

**中文标题**: Tokenisation is not in APX

**作者**: Violeta Kastreva, Philip Whittington, Dennis Komm

**中文摘要**: In this paper, we prove that, unless P=NP, no polynomial-time algorithm can find a tokeniser whose objective is within any constant factor of the optimum, meaning that tokenisation is not in the APX complexity class. We prove this for direct and bottom-up tokenisation, under both a compression or unigram log-likelihood objectives, and for binary or larger alphabets. We further show that, even over unary alphabets, direct tokenisation is APX-hard, and thus admits no polynomial-time approximation scheme.

### 3. Can image generation help visual reasoning? An empirical study

**中文标题**: Can image generation help visual reasoning? An empirical study

**作者**: Xueyang Yu, Yifan Jing, Taiming Lu

**中文摘要**: Language models think in words; then multimodal models might think in images. We test whether a generated helper improves the answer to a visual question. A model describes the helper it needs and then answers using the original and generated images together. Across 13 vision-language model and generator pairs, generation lowers mean accuracy by 3.7 percentage points, with declines on nine of ten tasks. Two unified models, BAGEL and Janus-Pro-7B, each lose accuracy on eight tasks. On VSTAR, adding a generated image to the same planning hint lowers accuracy by 10.47 points. Repeated image edits yield different outcomes across tasks. ChartQAPro and MathVista stay below their no-generation baselines, while ERQA gains three points after ten edits.

### 4. Attack Policy Selection can Mislead Monitor Evaluators

**中文标题**: Attack Policy Selection can Mislead Monitor Evaluators

**作者**: Charlie Griffin, Oliver Makins, Asa Cooper Stickland

**中文摘要**: AI monitors, classifiers that flag dangerous behaviour by AI agents, are often evaluated against transcripts generated by attack policies acting as proxies for dangerous behaviour in deployment. We evaluate an ex-production OpenAI monitor as a case study and find that measured false negative rate is highly sensitive to both the method used to generate attack policies and the level of optimisation applied within that method. This rate ranged from 0.5\% to 20.8\% across optimisation levels of a single method (reinforcement learning), and from 6.4\% to 23.0\% across methods at their best observed optimisation level (optimised prompting vs.\ transcript editing). Every attack policy was developed against proxy monitors only: all reported results show attacks transferring to the held-out GPT-5.4 target monitor. Our results suggest that monitor evasion evaluations can easily mislead: an evaluation using only basic attack methods may give false confidence that a monitor is strong.   The sensitivity to attack-policy choice raises a problem: interpreting any particular result as evidence about deployment performance requires justification. The transcripts produced by attack policies are proxies for the dangerous behaviour a monitor must flag in deployment. These proxies can introduce visible artefacts, such as reasoning about a ``side task'' absent from the instructions shown to the monitor, that affect monitor judgements. Given the sensitivity above, the choice of proxy can change an evaluation's conclusion. Reading these results as evidence about deployment therefore requires a separate legitimacy argument for why the proxies are informative, including any justification of conservativeness. Making convincing arguments remains an open problem.

### 5. A gene informed benchmark for BioScoring of pathology foundation models

**中文标题**: A gene informed benchmark for BioScoring of pathology foundation models

**作者**: Paul Vilars, Vincent Quoc-Huy Trinh, Maria Vakalopoulou

**中文摘要**: Pathology foundation models are usually compared by their performance on downstream tasks such as classification and retrieval. While such benchmarks measure how useful a model is, they provide no insight into the biology it represents. We introduce BioScore, a benchmark that quantifies how well an image encoder captures cell identities, with spatial transcriptomics of the same tissue as the reference. A sparse autoencoder splits the encoder's patch embeddings into sparse features, which we cluster into a fixed number of groups. BioScore then measures, for each of 52 cell identities defined by marker genes, how specifically one group follows the identity's expression over the tissue. Because the autoencoder training is fully unsupervised, a match shows that the model represents the cell identity from the image alone. We score 8 pathology foundation models and 3 natural-image models. Every pathology model scores above every natural-image model (0.299--0.371 \vs 0.214--0.232), and paired tests separate three tiers among the pathology models. Unlike a supervised probe, BioScore needs no labels, builds its reference from the data and scores even from few sections, yet it ranks the encoders as a probe does. Its per-identity breakdown indicates which cell identities each encoder captures or misses.
