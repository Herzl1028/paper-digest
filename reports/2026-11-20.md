### 1. RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering

**中文标题**: RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering

**作者**: Tuna Han Salih Meral, Kaan Oktay, Alec Helbling

**中文摘要**: Reinforcement learning (RL) post-training substantially improves the visual quality, text rendering, and compositional accuracy of diffusion transformers. Because capabilities such as rendering requested text or composing multiple objects are inherently prompt-dependent, a natural hypothesis is that improving them requires learning complex, input-dependent transformations. To isolate how much of these gains an input-independent shift explains, we probe each RL-tuned model with a deliberately simple parameterization: a fixed set of steering vectors added to the block outputs of the frozen base model. On SD3.5-M, these vectors hold 65x fewer parameters than the RL-tuned LoRA (0.29M against 18.8M). We fit them to match the velocity predictions of public RL-tuned checkpoints, with no reward in the objective. Although the vectors are identical for every prompt, token, and sampling seed, they recover 90.0-96.3% of the reward gains of six RL-tuned image models, and 89.8% and 111.9% on two RL-tuned video models. Initialized at zero and trained with online RL, the vectors alone exceed the reward gains of weight-based RL on text rendering (OCR) and human preference (PickScore) on SD3.5-M. A constant shift thus suffices for most of the gain of policy-gradient RL post-training: without new input-dependent transformations, it steers the pretrained generator toward what it can already compute.

### 2. PlayCraft: Designing Engaging Games from Agent Play Experience

**中文标题**: PlayCraft: Designing Engaging Games from Agent Play Experience

**作者**: Weihao Cheng

**中文摘要**: Recent AI agents can generate playable games, but these games are not necessarily engaging. Mastery is a key source of engagement, yet designing for it requires extensive human play and tuning. We propose PlayCraft, an automated framework that uses a vision-based agent to play games and draws on its detailed play experience to revise them for mastery. Starting from an initial game, PlayCraft alternates between playing and crafting. In the playing stage, a player agent watches the game screen, uses the controls, and pursues the game objective over repeated episodes while retaining memory. After each episode, it reports its progress, outcomes, decisions, and reflections. In the crafting stage, a crafter agent uses these reports to revise the game. It estimates player abilities, predicts performance at different proficiency levels, and compares those predictions with a target learning progression. It diagnoses the game and addresses the gaps by giving players meaningful opportunities to improve through better decisions and execution. We evaluate games in fresh runs with player agents across three intelligence settings, measuring learning gain and learning slope. Compared with the initial games, PlayCraft yields larger learning gains and steeper learning slopes in most games. It also outperforms the baselines on the macro-average of each metric at every intelligence setting.

### 3. TideCache: Efficient Hybrid LLM Serving with Transparent Cache Management and Layer-Wise Streaming

**中文标题**: TideCache: Efficient Hybrid LLM Serving with Transparent Cache Management and Layer-Wise Streaming

**作者**: Jinbin Luo, Xiaozhe Yao, Shang Yang

**中文摘要**: Hybrid architectures have become mainstream among open-weight language models. Their global-attention and bounded-state layers maintain heterogeneous caches whose demands vary with context length and concurrency. We present TideCache, a serving system on CPU--GPU superchips with three components for allocating GPU memory among caches and managing their residency across GPU and host memory. The Transparent Cache Pool redistributes physical pages among cache components behind fixed virtual addresses, preserving cache layouts and backend interfaces. The Cache Streamer uses computation intervals provided by bounded-state layers to prefetch the next global-attention layer's prefix into GPU slots shared across global-attention layers, reducing GPU residency while overlapping layer-wise transfers with computation. Generated KV remains resident. The Request Scheduler selects resident or streamed execution and triggers capacity adjustments according to request size and queue state. Together, they let HBM released by streaming accommodate other cache components and additional requests. Implemented in SGLang and evaluated on GB300 and GH200 across seven model/platform configurations, TideCache completes up to 2.04x as many agent turns, reduces overall P50 per-token latency by 20--59%, and improves agent P50 TTFT by 1.40--3.05x compared with SGLang with HiCache.

### 4. One Prior, Two Dynamics: Repurposing Video Diffusion Transformers for Self-Supervised Latent Action Learning

**中文标题**: One Prior, Two Dynamics: Repurposing Video Diffusion Transformers for Self-Supervised Latent Action Learning

**作者**: Yuxin Jiang, Wenqiang Sun, Jiaqi Li

**中文摘要**: Videos reveal how the world changes, but rarely the actions that cause those changes. Latent action learning recovers such supervision from visual transitions, yet often learns the underlying dynamics largely from scratch. We ask whether a pretrained video generator can instead provide the prior for both inverse and forward dynamics. We introduce O$^{2}$LaF, built on a simple principle: one prior, two dynamics. We initialize both models from the same pretrained video DiT and adapt them to their distinct roles: a feed-forward transition encoder with learnable action queries for inverse dynamics, and a causal, action-conditioned video generator for forward dynamics. By controlling information access and optimizing both dynamics end-to-end through a compact bottleneck, O$^{2}$LaF learns transferable actions from unlabeled videos. Across navigation and robotic manipulation, O$^{2}$LaF sets a new state of the art in few-shot action probing and enables stronger zero-shot action transfer across visual contexts and embodiments. Beyond generative video priors, we further extend our framework to predictive representation priors such as V-JEPA2, achieving competitive performance. Our findings suggest a broader paradigm for latent-action learning: repurposing pretrained video models to discover transferable actions and predict their consequences.

### 5. Bio-PM-Guided Synthetic IMU Generation

**中文标题**: Bio-PM-Guided Synthetic IMU Generation

**作者**: Deepali Singh, Deepesh Parpani, Aviral Gupta

**中文摘要**: Collecting and labeling inertial measurement unit (IMU) data for human activity recognition (HAR) is expensive, and models trained on one group of subjects often fail to generalize to unseen individuals. Even with balanced data, classifiers struggle to distinguish dynamically similar activity classes and benefit from targeted augmentation. We explore whether synthetic IMU data generated in the representation space of a pretrained biosignal encoder, Bio-PM \cite{b1}, can serve as a practical augmentation source. Rather than synthesizing raw signals, we leverage Bio-PM's frozen encoder to convert 10-second IMU windows into token embedding sequences and train class-conditional decoders in this space: (1) an autoregressive transformer decoder that predicts tokens sequentially, and (2) a diffusion decoder that learns the token distribution through a denoising objective. We analyze generation realism through distributional metrics and embedding-space visualizations, evaluate downstream utility through leave-one-subject-out HAR classification, and present a PatchDecoder for approximate raw waveform reconstruction. Our diffusion decoder achieves a Train-on-Synthetic-Test-on-Real (TSTR) macro-F1 of 0.68, demonstrating that generated tokens are realistic enough to train a classifier that transfers to real data. Combining synthetic-token augmentation with a 320-dimensional gravity-aware feature representation raises LOSO macro-F1 from 0.729 to 0.809 over a real-only baseline across 51 subjects on the WISDM benchmark, with statistically significant gains concentrated in the most challenging activity classes. These results demonstrate that high-fidelity generation in a pretrained encoder's representation space is achievable and practically useful for cross-subject HAR augmentation.
