### 1. Pruning Autonomous Driving VLAs: What to Preserve for Reasoning and Action

**中文标题**: Pruning Autonomous Driving VLAs: What to Preserve for Reasoning and Action

**作者**: Seongchan Kim, Soowon Son, Sangoh Kim

**中文摘要**: Reasoning vision-language-action (VLA) models for autonomous driving use chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning to guide action generation, but incur substantial memory and computational costs. We study structured pruning for these models, focusing on preserving both scene understanding and trajectory prediction. Our analysis of Taylor importance in Alpamayo-1.5-10B shows that reasoning and trajectory objectives assign different importance to the same vision-language model (VLM) structures. In the action expert, MLP channels exhibit more stable importance rankings across denoising steps than query heads, and a small fraction of channels accounts for most of the measured importance. Based on these findings, we propose MoOP, which combines importance ranks from both objectives for VLM pruning and aggregates stepwise importance to select a fixed subset of expert MLP channels. At 24.0% total parameter reduction, VLM-only pruning with MoOP performs comparably to or slightly better than the strongest baselines in driving, while retaining 94.0% of the dense model's LingoQA score, far above all other methods. At a higher reduction of 30.0%, MoOP degrades much less than every other method, as it is the only one whose reasoning does not collapse. Removing 93.75% of expert MLP channels increases total parameter reduction to 39.4%, with nearly unchanged open-loop errors relative to VLM-only pruning and competitive closed-loop performance. The resulting model achieves a 1.37x end-to-end inference speedup and 36.6% lower peak memory than the dense model, without post-pruning fine-tuning.

### 2. Let Vision-Language Models Generate Images through Their Representations

**中文标题**: Let Vision-Language Models Generate Images through Their Representations

**作者**: JB Kim, Byungwoo Jeon, Kyungmin Lee

**中文摘要**: Recent unified multimodal models aim to combine visual understanding and generation within a single model, yet effectively integrating the two capabilities remains challenging. Leveraging pretrained vision-language models offers a way to build on already learned multimodal knowledge, but making this knowledge effectively accessible to visual generation remains nontrivial. We find that a central challenge lies in the heterogeneous visual representations used for understanding and generation. To address this challenge, we introduce Integral, which converts a pretrained vision-language model into a unified multimodal model by constructing a shared visual representation space across the two capabilities. Integral implements this design through two complementary components. Abstraction Fusion constructs shared visual tokens by aggregating hierarchical representations from the frozen vision encoder, while Block-wise MoT introduces a lightweight generative stream that interacts with the vision-language model only at block boundaries. Our approach effectively preserves the pretrained understanding capability while extending it to strong visual generation. Controlled comparisons further demonstrate that sharing visual representations between understanding and generation substantially improves generative learning compared with using a separate visual representation space, despite comparable reconstruction fidelity. These results suggest that representations learned by pretrained vision-language models already provide an effective foundation for visual generation.

### 3. TowerBridge: Bridging the Dual-Encoder Modality Gap via Weight-Space Programming

**中文标题**: TowerBridge: Bridging the Dual-Encoder Modality Gap via Weight-Space Programming

**作者**: Hani Alomari, Md Atabuzzaman, Chris Thomas

**中文摘要**: CLIP-style dual encoders map images and text through two separate towers into a shared embedding space, where the two modalities occupy distinct regions separated by a modality gap. Retrieval fails when wrong matches, or hard negatives, outrank the correct one, and the gap is the usual suspect. Yet removing it post hoc degrades retrieval on every CLIP checkpoint we test. We introduce a paired statistic that identifies directions where the two towers agree more on hard negatives than on matched pairs. Mildly attenuating these directions improves retrieval while largely preserving the gap. We arrive at this correction through weight-space programming: guided by the statistic, an LLM agent inspects both towers and writes closed-form weight edits without backpropagation. Free to edit any component, the agent improves COCO test retrieval on all 13 CLIP checkpoints and edits an output projection in 12 of the 13. We distill and refine its recurring correction into TowerBridge, a closed-form recipe motivated by a first-order analysis of the hard-negative margin. Computed in seconds from 25,000 labelled pairs, it requires no model-specific tuning and leaves the architecture and inference cost unchanged. TowerBridge improves COCO test retrieval by 16.8 RSUM and Flickr30k test retrieval by 6.6 on average across all 21 evaluated CLIP checkpoints, including 8 unseen by the agent, while limiting any drop in ImageNet zero-shot accuracy to 0.5 points. It also reduces hubness on all 21 checkpoints and improves retrieval on 4 of 5 dual encoders from other families.

### 4. Euclid Data Release 1 (DR1). Learning machine learning: Investigating the complementarity and biases of strong gravitational lens finding networks on Euclid data

**中文标题**: Euclid Data Release 1 (DR1). Learning machine learning: Investigating the complementarity and biases of strong gravitational lens finding networks on Euclid data

**作者**:  Euclid Collaboration, S. H. Vincken, N. E. P. Lines

**中文摘要**: The Euclid Data Release 1 (DR1) strong lens search presents ECLIPSE, the largest catalogue of strong gravitational lens candidates to date, with >15 000 systems identified across 1900 deg2 of sky. It was constructed using five neural networks applied to approximately 68 million galaxies, followed by citizen science and expert visual inspection. In this work, we compare how the independently developed networks use image information to identify their selection of strong lens candidates. The search combines convolutional neural networks and a vision transformer, using the same pool of data but with different image processing and training choices. We investigate why these networks find different systems and what that means for the completeness of our catalogue. We evaluate all networks on a 50 deg2 calibration set and their application to the full DR1 sample. We find that the networks score candidates very differently, and that combining them recovers over 500 lens candidates within our calibration set at an inspection budget of 2000 objects. On the full DR1 sample, we show that individual networks have distinct biases: some networks favour systems with a larger Einstein radius while others are more sensitive to compact configurations. We also investigate network preferences in terms of lens light profile, mass distribution geometry, photometric redshift, and magnitudes. These findings provide a detailed analysis of how neural network selection biases have shaped the Euclid DR1 ECLIPSE catalogue. The results inform how networks should be combined in future Euclid data releases and upcoming wide-field surveys such as LSST or Roman to maximise both completeness and diversity of the recovered lens population.

### 5. Language Models Steer on Injected Uncertainty Tokens, but Fail to Self-Monitor In Situ

**中文标题**: Language Models Steer on Injected Uncertainty Tokens, but Fail to Self-Monitor In Situ

**作者**: Daniel Theron

**中文摘要**: Prompting language models to verbalize stepwise numerical confidence during generation is an increasingly popular approach for triggering backtracking and error correction in agentic reasoning. This strategy presupposes two capabilities: (1) in-situ monitoring fidelity (does confidence drop when an error occurs?) and (2) downstream steering control (does an uncertainty token causally steer the model to repair its error?). We evaluated four open-weights models (Qwen2.5-7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B, and Phi-4, 14.7B) across 15,200 math benchmark attempts, isolating 2,017 reasoning errors within each model's capability frontier. Applying 27,568 counterfactual continuations across these failure points, we demonstrate a consistent decoupling between monitoring and control.   On monitoring, all four models fail to detect their own errors. Verbalized confidence does not distinguish mistake steps from sound reasoning, yielding near-chance within-position AUROCs between 0.500 and 0.561. Even at a model's first reasoning error, trajectory confidence remains saturated at 95.1% to 99.9%, with noticeable drops ($\ge 10$ points) appearing on fewer than 9% of errors in Llama and under 1.6% in the other three models. In contrast, models respond readily to external uncertainty: injecting a low-confidence tag (10%) at the error step significantly increases downstream correction across all models (+3.7 to +22.5 points over format-matched 95% certainty; all $p \le 0.0022$), reaching up to 38.5% under active rethinking instructions. However, injecting doubt onto already-correct steps causes models to discard valid reasoning, reducing solve rates by up to 22.8 points. Inference-time self-correction is thus bottlenecked by in-situ error detection rather than downstream repair capacity: models readily steer toward recovery when prompted with doubt, yet fail to self-monitor during generation.
