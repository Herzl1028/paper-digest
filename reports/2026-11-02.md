### 1. Latent Reasoning in Continuous Space for Unified Multimodal Models

**中文标题**: Latent Reasoning in Continuous Space for Unified Multimodal Models

**作者**: Byungwoo Jeon, Yoonwoo Jeong, Hyunseok Lee

**中文摘要**: Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) achieve remarkable success on diverse multimodal understanding and generation tasks, yet they still struggle to effectively leverage language-based chain-of-thought reasoning for image generation. As a result, existing reasoning-augmented UMMs often fail to inherit the test-time scaling and fine-grained controllability observed in large language models. We attribute this failure to a fundamental modality mismatch: reasoning is performed in a discrete token space, whereas image generation operates in a continuous space. To bridge this gap, we introduce a Latent Reasoning framework in Continuous space (LARC), which alleviates the token-level bottleneck by reasoning over continuous hidden states. Specifically, the training framework consists of two stages: (i) curriculum supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and (ii) information-gain reinforcement learning (RL). First, the curriculum SFT stage gradually converts token-level reasoning into latent reasoning. Subsequently, the information-gain RL stage self-evolves latent reasoning without ground truth supervision. Experiments across diverse text-to-image generation benchmarks show that LARC consistently improves generation quality, prompt following, and compositional fidelity. Notably, LARC achieves state-of-the-art performance on GenEval with a 7%p gain over BAGEL and demonstrates clear test-time scaling behavior, outperforming token-based generation under both parallel and sequential test-time scaling strategies.

### 2. Latent Sparse Attention: Towards Subquadratic Ultra-Long Context Attention

**中文标题**: Latent Sparse Attention: Towards Subquadratic Ultra-Long Context Attention

**作者**: Ales Kubicek, Andrea Caciolai, Liangzu Peng

**中文摘要**: Agentic workflows are increasingly shifting towards long-horizon tasks, such as open-ended scientific work, where models benefit from long context windows. However, at ultra-long context (above 1M tokens), full attention is prohibitively expensive. A promising approach is sparse attention, in which a dedicated indexer selects a subset of keys for the attention. Yet current methods of indexing either still have quadratic cost in context size or lose accuracy at ultra-long context scale. We address this with Latent Sparse Attention (LSA), an efficient attention method that maintains accuracy at ultra-long context. In LSA, keys are hierarchically clustered in a learned latent space, and queries score the hierarchy of clusters instead of scoring every key, making prefill, decode and training faster at long context. We propose two variants, Distilled-LSA (D-LSA), which can be applied to pre-trained models via distillation, and Native-LSA (N-LSA), which is trained natively on the next-token loss alone. On GLM-5.3-FLASH (QWEN-3.8-27B) at 10M tokens, D-LSA speeds up one attention layer by 22.7x (72.4x) in prefill and 4.6x (42.5x) in decode over its original sparse attention (full attention), while keeping similar accuracy across a variety of tasks. N-LSA matches the accuracy of full attention with a training speedup of 1.6x per layer at 128k tokens. LSA offers practical and efficient attention for models to benefit from ultra-long context.

### 3. Hierarchical World Models with Implicit Dynamics

**中文标题**: Hierarchical World Models with Implicit Dynamics

**作者**: Gaoyue Zhou, Yvonne Wu, Zichen Jeff Cui

**中文摘要**: Planning with world models enables test-time adaptation for embodied agents, offering superior generalization over direct policy learning. However, long-horizon tasks remain hindered by cumulative errors and search complexity. Existing hierarchical methods often use rigid macro-actions or latent skills, leading to brittle representations and limited fine-grained control. We introduce ID-WM (Implicit Dynamics World Models), a hierarchical world model that decouples state-space reachability from local control. Our framework utilizes a high-level model to sample feasible transitions across the state manifold and a low-level world model as an inverse dynamics solver to ground these transitions into precise actions. This allows the planner to identify reachable subgoals without being constrained by low-level execution details. By prioritizing local physical feasibility over global behavioral patterns, ID-WM synthesizes novel long-horizon plans from short segments, transcending the specific behavioral modes seen during training. We demonstrate its effectiveness across three navigation and manipulation suites, achieving a 38.5% improvement over state-of-the-art policy and diffusion-based planners. Crucially, by leveraging this hierarchical structure to mitigate compounding errors, our approach yields a 135% performance gain over single-level world model MPC, particularly in complex, unseen configurations for long-horizon tasks. Videos can be viewed at https://id-wm.github.io

### 4. RoboArchitect: Automating the Design of Robotic Agents with Persistent Memory

**中文标题**: RoboArchitect: Automating the Design of Robotic Agents with Persistent Memory

**作者**: Shengguang Wu, Suning Huang, Cherie Ho

**中文摘要**: Recent work shows that a frozen robot policy performs far better inside an agent harness, where control code decides when to query the policy, consult vision tools, or command the arm directly. Yet today these harnesses are built by hand and redesigned for each new robot or benchmark. We show that harness design can be automated, offering a strong alternative to finetuning on a novel setup. In RoboArchitect, a coding model observes a robot's rollouts, diagnoses failures, and iteratively revises the harness around the frozen policy. The harness evolves on a set of episodes for its optimization and is evaluated on held-out scenes and tasks. Without updating any model weights, RoboArchitect raises average success from 9.7% to 43.3% on unseen scenes (4.5x) and from 12.7% to 40.2% on unseen tasks (3.2x), across multiple benchmarks and policies. The gains are largest where the benchmark is far from the policy's training data: wrapping a general-purpose policy, the evolved harness yields a specialist that often outperforms even the finetuned version. Intuitive control strategies are automatically designed, such as lifting a jammed arm clear or reaching in horizontally when an obstacle blocks the way from above. The harness also evolves its own persistent memory, carrying learned experience, such as effective grasp depths, into later decisions. These results suggest that substantial headroom in robotic manipulation lies in the agent harness, and its design can be effectively automated and optimized without weight updates.

### 5. Counting on Dummies: Privacy Amplification with Observable Counts and Truncated Batches

**中文标题**: Counting on Dummies: Privacy Amplification with Observable Counts and Truncated Batches

**作者**: Vitaly Feldman, Audra McMillan, Kunal Talwar

**中文摘要**: In many practical deployments of Differentially Private Stochastic Gradient Descent (DP-SGD), the server processes data in batches. Randomly sampling these batches allows for better privacy bounds via privacy amplification results. Most sampling schemes with provable privacy amplification, including Poisson sampling and random allocation, result in variable batch sizes. The privacy guarantee of these schemes requires that the batch sizes are not revealed. In distributed settings, the server performing the aggregation or any party observing the communication traffic may learn these batch sizes. Furthermore, in both centralized and distributed settings, memory and computation constraints may require each batch to be of some maximum size $s$, necessitating truncation of the batch when it exceeds this limit. It is known that this truncation can result in a significant increase in privacy loss. In this work, we address both of these challenges via a simple mechanism that uses a random number of \emph{dummy points} to privatize the batch size and maintain the privacy guarantee even under truncation. We demonstrate that our mechanism is easy to implement in a distributed setting, and provide a general analysis of this mechanism for both Poisson sampling and random allocation. Our analysis is designed to support efficient and relatively tight privacy accounting. It also effectively amplifies the privacy of the count releases by subsampling, making the expected number of dummy points asymptotically smaller than what is needed to directly privatize the (sensitivity-one) batch sizes with non-negative noise. For Poisson sampling with truncated batches, we demonstrate empirically that our mechanism achieves better privacy--efficiency tradeoffs than the direct accounting for truncation recently given by (Ganesh, 2025).
