### 1. APTree: Extended Behavior Trees for Adaptive and Scalable Automated Robotic Task Planning in Construction

**中文标题**: APTree: Extended Behavior Trees for Adaptive and Scalable Automated Robotic Task Planning in Construction

**作者**: Shermin Sherkat, Thomas Wortmann, Andreas Wortmann

**中文摘要**: Task planning for robots in off-site construction prefabrication is primarily manual, leading to planning inefficiencies and reluctance to adopt robotic solutions. Existing approaches provide limited support for scalable, modular automated task planning and localized runtime replanning. This paper proposes APTree, a model-driven framework that integrates automated task planning with modular task execution using behavior trees as the execution model. APTree repeatedly applies and coordinates heterogeneous symbolic planners to automatically generate and compose modular task plans at runtime. To support this approach, we develop a domain-specific language for the formal specification and validation of task plans, together with a compatible execution engine. We evaluate APTree on the real-world livMatS Biomimetic Shell pavilion, where it models the construction robotic fabrication domain, detects injected specification and planning errors at design time and runtime, and automatically generates large-scale task plans by coordinating three symbolic planners across numerous planning modules. A machine-checked formal analysis further shows that composed execution and local replanning are safe under explicitly stated assumptions, including planner soundness and non-interference of generated plans, providing practitioners with explicit conditions for dependable plan composition. APTree thus enables construction practitioners to leverage automated task planning without requiring detailed knowledge of symbolic planning techniques or APTree's internal implementation.

### 2. Lost in Implicit Routing: How LLM Agents Fail to Load Relevant Skills at Scale

**中文标题**: Lost in Implicit Routing: How LLM Agents Fail to Load Relevant Skills at Scale

**作者**: Jinlin Liu, Wei Chen, Xiaojin Zhang

**中文摘要**: Reusable skills let LLM agents load task-specific instructions on demand, but they make execution depend on implicit routing over skill metadata. We test whether this design scales by holding SkillsBench tasks and their golden skills fixed while adding irrelevant skills. We find skill routing collapse: agents increasingly load fewer or no skills, even though the relevant skills remain available. With 1,000 irrelevant skills, loaded-skill recall falls from 73.6% to 27.4% and the zero-load rate rises from 9.3% to 51.6%, whereas explicit selection from the same library retains 73.9% recall. Loading other skills, including plausible alternatives to golden skills, is too rare to explain this decline. Masking only the descriptions of irrelevant skills largely restores skill loading, yet with the metadata intact, explicitly asking agents to plan skill use still yields lower recall than preselection. These findings point to semantic interference from descriptions and instruction interference when skill choice competes with task execution. We also evaluate explicit preselection as a practical mitigation, including its coverage and latency trade-offs. Our analysis informs skill protocols and evaluation for agents with growing skill libraries.

### 3. The Geometry of Language Model Logits: Generic Structure and Learned Departures

**中文标题**: The Geometry of Language Model Logits: Generic Structure and Learned Departures

**作者**: Swathi Shree Narashiman, Aravind Ramana Venkatramanan, Nambi Seshadri

**中文摘要**: Autoregressive language models produce, at every prediction step, a vocabulary-sized logit vector whose structure determines the conditional next-token distribution. We empirically identify that the ordered vocabulary-head logits follow a remarkably consistent log-linear relation   \[ z_{(r)} \approx μ- α\log r. \] across prediction contexts, architectures, and scales, emerging progressively during pretraining. To our knowledge, this is the first systematic analysis of the logit geometry underlying the distributional assumptions used in the prior decoding work. While controlled order-statistic baselines spanning bounded, light-tailed, and heavier-tailed distributions can largely explain the observed log-linear geometry, they do not explain the systematic shift in the slope $α$: trained language models exhibit $α\approx 1.4$--$1.7$, sitting well above the initialization baseline ($α\approx 0.2$) . Moreover, $α$ correlates with predictive performance, suggesting that it captures learned structure beyond generic extreme-value effects.

### 4. Dual-Domain Sparse-View 4D CBCT Reconstruction with Spatial-Temporal Consistency Learning

**中文标题**: Dual-Domain Sparse-View 4D CBCT Reconstruction with Spatial-Temporal Consistency Learning

**作者**: Qi Lai, YuTong He

**中文摘要**: Four-dimensional cone-beam computed tomography (4D CBCT) provides phase-resolved volumetric images for image-guided radiotherapy. Yet good reconstruction quality usually needs many projections. This increases scan time and radiation dose. We propose STCNet, a sparse-view 4D CBCT reconstruction framework that uses spatial-temporal consistency learning. STCNet reconstructs high-quality images from only 16 projections in each respiratory phase. STCNet integrates physics-driven positional encoding to enforce geometric consistency in the projection domain and introduces temporal encoding in latent space to capture respiratory motion dynamics across phases to capture dynamic respiratory motion across phases. A dual-domain collaborative strategy jointly optimizes both projection and image domains, enhancing structural fidelity and temporal coherence. We tested STCNet on the TCIA-LUNG dataset and a CIRS thorax phantom dataset. STCNet performed better than conventional methods and recent deep learning baselines in image quality, artifact reduction, and structural preservation. On TCIA-LUNG, STCNet reached PSNR values of 33.311, 32.695, and 32.658 dB at phases 2, 5, and 8. The corresponding SSIM values were 0.845, 0.823, and 0.825. On CIRS, STCNet achieved PSNR values up to 36.497 dB and SSIM values up to 0.877. These results support STCNet as a promising framework for low-dose, phase-resolved 4D CBCT reconstruction in image-guided radiotherapy.

### 5. Two For The Price Of One: Curvature-Aware Pruning Preserves Influence Functions Too

**中文标题**: Two For The Price Of One: Curvature-Aware Pruning Preserves Influence Functions Too

**作者**: Mehdi Makni, Natalia Ponomareva, Rahul Mazumder

**中文摘要**: Post-training pruning enables efficient deployment, but conventional methods do not explicitly preserve internal-gradient alignment between sparse and dense models. We study novel connections between downstream performance of compressed models and the ability to retain gradient information via influence-function preservation. Central to this connection is a curvature-aware objective that accounts for both layer activations and backpropagated gradients. Despite its promise, this objective remains underexplored in pruning due to a lack of efficient solvers. We introduce $\texttt{CACTUS}$, a novel ADMM-based optimizer supporting both pruning and sparse-plus-low-rank decompositions of pre-trained weights. Compared to LLM-Surgeon which targets the same objective, $\texttt{CACTUS}$ can achieve over an order-of-magnitude speedup. At $50\%$ pruning of Qwen3-4B, $\texttt{CACTUS}$ reduces the C4 perplexity gap to the dense model by $45\%$, improves compressed-to-dense per-sample gradient cosine similarity on WikiText-2 from $0.670$ to $0.755$, and runs over $28\times$ faster than LLM-Surgeon on substantially weaker hardware. By preserving influence functions, $\texttt{CACTUS}$ supports effective data selection. On Llama-3.2-3B, fine-tuning a fresh dense model on the $5\%$ of data selected by $\texttt{CACTUS}$ achieves $49.44\%$ accuracy on BIG-Bench Hard, compared with $47.22\%$ using ALPS and $49.54\%$ using dense-model selection. Finally, integrating $\texttt{CACTUS}$ with LoGRA, a recent gradient-projection strategy, on Qwen3-4B with MLP layers pruned to 2:4 sparsity and a rank-64 correction retains a $0.711$ cosine alignment with dense LoGRA-projected C4 gradients.
