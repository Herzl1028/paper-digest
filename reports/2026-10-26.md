### 1. SceneBloom: Agentic Indoor Environment Generation with Realistic and Diverse Layouts for Embodied Learning

**中文标题**: SceneBloom: Agentic Indoor Environment Generation with Realistic and Diverse Layouts for Embodied Learning

**作者**: Yuhan Liu, Lianhui Qin, Shenji Wan

**中文摘要**: Training generalizable embodied agents requires realistic, varied, and simulation-ready indoor environments. Language-guided methods support semantic planning and object-level reasoning, but precise spatial grounding remains difficult. Visual generative models offer rich priors over plausible arrangements, yet their outputs require geometric reasoning to become physically valid 3D scenes. We introduce SceneBloom, an agentic framework that combines visual layout sampling with contract-guided 3D compilation. A hierarchical sampler uses diffusion priors to generate furniture arrangements within functional zones. A compiler then uses relations extracted from each proposal to guide geometric fitting and local pose correction. Across five room types, SceneBloom attains the highest visual quality and physical stability among the compared methods and is the only method with no detected mesh collisions and out-of-bounds objects. Its layouts are also closer to the distribution of real rooms than those of all compared baselines. Object navigation policies trained in these realistic scenes achieve a higher success rate in held-out real scans than those trained on an equal number of real training scans. Code is available at https://github.com/Tinaliu0123/scenebloom.

### 2. RobustRLlib: A Unified Library and Benchmark for Robust Reinforcement Learning Algorithms

**中文标题**: RobustRLlib: A Unified Library and Benchmark for Robust Reinforcement Learning Algorithms

**作者**: Xi Chen, Fengqi Hu, Zeyu Shen

**中文摘要**: Robust reinforcement learning (RL) is critical for reliable real-world deployment, where policies may fail under diverse shifts and disruptions. Existing robust RL algorithms offer a rich set of mechanisms and principles, yet two major barriers limit their broader potential: limited algorithm reusability and an unclear empirical capability boundary. We introduce RobustRLlib, an algorithm-centric library and benchmark that addresses both challenges. It integrates 17 representative robust online, offline, and safe RL algorithms under a unified interface for substantially improved reusability, together with an enhanced evaluation platform spanning diverse real-world shifts and advanced humanoid and vision-language-action tasks. Systematic evaluation over 273 perturbation configurations provides an up-to-date capability map of current robust RL algorithms, revealing where they remain effective and where substantial robustness gaps persist. As an open-source, user-friendly platform, RobustRLlib offers a reproducible and extensible codebase for applying existing methods to new problems and developing new robust RL algorithms.

### 3. DexPort: A Hand-Held Skill Interface for Dexterous Hand Data Collection

**中文标题**: DexPort: A Hand-Held Skill Interface for Dexterous Hand Data Collection

**作者**: Amber Xie, Jiawei Fu, Xiaofeng Guo

**中文摘要**: Collecting high-quality demonstrations remains a central bottleneck for learning dexterous manipulation. Teleoperation records executable robot actions, but it requires a complete robot setup and places the burden of coordinating many finger joints on the operator. Human demonstrations are easier to collect, but their motions must be estimated and retargeted to the robot, and they do not directly provide robot actions. In this paper, we introduce DexPort, a portable data-collection interface for high-DoF robot hands. It uses the same hand hardware for collection and deployment, avoiding a hand embodiment gap, and records both commanded joint targets and measured joint states. Our key observation is that a small set of skills can support a wide range of dexterous tasks. During collection, the operator moves the hand-held interface to specify the robot wrist motion and selects or adjusts skills that coordinate the finger motion. This removes the need to control each joint independently while retaining behaviors such as precise grasping and in-hand manipulation. We collect demonstrations across several real-world tasks and in unstructured environments without a robot arm. We then use behavior cloning to train visuomotor policies directly on the recorded wrist motions and hand commands, without skill labels. We evaluate across 4 tasks: pick-and-place with diverse objects, screwing with in-hand tool use, drawer stowage which requires simultaneous grasp and pulling, and a bimanual scanning task. We collect over 2,500 demonstrations with our portable device, in both laboratory and in-the-wild settings. Across our tasks, we see an improvement from 11% to 64% when using recorded robot commands instead of states, and an improvement from 36% to 78% for task success when using in-the-wild data.

### 4. Distributed Quantum-Centric Supercomputing with Multi-Layered Generative Learning for Intermolecular Interactions

**中文标题**: Distributed Quantum-Centric Supercomputing with Multi-Layered Generative Learning for Intermolecular Interactions

**作者**: Chayan Patra, Prayas Ghosh, Adwait Patkhedkar

**中文摘要**: Quantum-centric supercomputing (QCSC) approaches, such as sample-based quantum diagonalization (SQD), combine quantum processors with high-performance computers (HPC), offering a promising route toward solving challenging electronic structure problems. However, implementation of QCSC approaches to large active space calculations is constrained by the associated number of qubits, deep quantum circuits, sampling cost, and large-scale Hamiltonian diagonalization. To overcome these limitations, we introduce a distributed QCSC framework that decomposes complex molecular problems into parallel sub-problems requiring fewer qubits, shallower circuits, and reduced shot budgets. A physically motivated, two-step aggregation scheme then reconstructs the global solution: first, a computational bit assignment strategy optimizes a cost function to appropriately map local bit indices to global ones, yielding a mapped tensor product state (MTPS); second, an inter-fragment correlation recovery module restores missing cross-fragment interactions. Herein, we employ a multi-layered generative machine learning architecture, starting with utilizing the recently proposed physics-informed generative SQD (PIGen-SQD) as a distributed QCSC solver, followed by a secondary generative module to model configuration distributions and identify dominant sectors of the global Hilbert space. For water and formamide dimers, our method achieves up to $95\%$ reduction in the diagonalization dimension while enhancing energy accuracy by an order of magnitude over conventional SQD. Furthermore, application to the water hexamer ($\mathrm{(H_2O)_6}$) cage successfully decomposes a 120-qubit problem into six 20-qubit monomeric subsystems, and stitches the monomers under a dimeric approximation, demonstrating excellent agreement with the reference classical methods.

### 5. Flownomaly: Single-Step Conditional-Mean Recovery for Few-Shot Anomaly Detection

**中文标题**: Flownomaly: Single-Step Conditional-Mean Recovery for Few-Shot Anomaly Detection

**作者**: Bernardo Perrone Ribeiro, Erkut Akdag, Egor Bondarev

**中文摘要**: Few-shot industrial anomaly detection requires modeling category-specific normality from only a handful of defect-free images. Common approaches tie inference to stored exemplars, impose rigid linear structure, or risk reproducing anomalous content. Flownomaly instead casts the problem as normal-counterpart recovery in frozen DINOv2 feature space. Given normal supports, a velocity field is learned through conditional flow matching over frozen patch tokens to transport Gaussian noise toward normal features. Along straight paths, a single recovery step using one evaluation of the optimal field yields the posterior mean of the clean normal token without trajectory integration. The operating time governs how strongly recovery modifies a query token. With no anomalous validation samples available for tuning, a normal-only Jacobian gain-to-noise criterion selects this time from the supports alone. Squared residuals between query tokens and their recovered counterparts yield anomaly signals for both dense localization and image-level detection. Across 1-, 2-, and 4-shot benchmarks on MVTec-AD and VisA, together with sample-shot evaluations on Real-IAD, Flownomaly achieves state-of-the-art performance against recent category-specific methods. In the 1-shot setting, image- and pixel-level AUROC reach 97.8% and 98.0% on MVTec-AD and 95.3% and 98.7% on VisA, with a 2.1-point image-level AUROC improvement over the strongest baseline on VisA. Code is available at https://github.com/b-rbmp/Flownomaly.
