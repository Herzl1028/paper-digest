### 1. On Agentic Manipulation Policies

**中文标题**: On Agentic Manipulation Policies

**作者**: Chen Chen, Omid Bagherieh, Naveen Nikamanth Appiah Balaji

**中文摘要**: Recent work on robot manipulation has focused on specialist policies trained on demonstrations. We show that generalist AI models can control manipulators more reliably than specialist policies such as vision-language-action models (VLAs) and world-action models (WAMs). We systematically analyze agentic manipulation policies (AMPs), in which a generalist AI model commands the manipulator directly, adapting its actions as the task unfolds. We present AMP1, a reference harness that runs unchanged across single-arm, bimanual, and mobile manipulation. AMP1 achieves state-of-the-art results on the RoboLab-120 and CaP-Gym benchmarks, with no demonstrations or task-specific training. On RoboLab-120, AMP1 reaches an 85.6\% success rate zero-shot, compared to 49.2\% and 42.9\% for leading VLA and WAM policies. The agent succeeds zero-shot in 104 of 108 real-world trials spanning twelve tasks and three robot instances, reducing failure rates by 20x and 15x relative to strong VLA and WAM baselines. The basic harness supplies no grasping, pushing, or placing skills; the agent develops its own strategies, such as searching for hidden objects, using objects as tools, and calibrating its cameras. With the addition of a persistent memory store, the agent can also learn from experience: an augmented harness with a memory representation that is carried across episodes lowers the failure rate on RoboDojo bimanual manipulation tasks by 4.6x relative to zero-shot deployment. Our analysis suggests that AMPs are substantially more general and robust than specialist manipulation policies, providing a promising foundation for robot manipulation.

### 2. CPU-ARC: Agentic Reasoning Caching for on-CPU Reasoning Reuse

**中文标题**: CPU-ARC: Agentic Reasoning Caching for on-CPU Reasoning Reuse

**作者**: Peter Belcak, Greg Heinrich, Saurav Muralidharan

**中文摘要**: Large language models reinvent the wheel all the time. They conflate task-solving with the execution of the solutions, which forces them to solve every problem from scratch. We show that once a task has been reasoned through, the application of its solution to a new problem instance can be done very efficiently and with little involvement from language models. We introduce agentic reasoning caching, a method for constructing robust CPU-executable agentic reasoning caches (ARCs) from LLM reasoning traces. ARCs show on-par performance with frontier LLMs across a range of difficult mathematical and scientific tasks, are fully explainable, continually trainable, robust to input perturbations, and 16-291x cheaper to invoke than the source model.

### 3. SciCheck: A Framework for Verifying the Integrity of Deep Learning Training

**中文标题**: SciCheck: A Framework for Verifying the Integrity of Deep Learning Training

**作者**: Alexander Nguyen, Dylan Gresham, Gaby G. Dagher

**中文摘要**: Deep learning training is difficult to verify within a scientific workflow: its architecture, hyperparameters, and procedure are rarely fixed until training has been attempted, and two honest runs never produce the same weights, so recomputation cannot serve as a check. We present SciCheck, a framework in which the scientist explores training configurations freely and posts a blueprint to a provenance ledger for each candidate they want verified, and a verifying scientist judges the resulting run in two layers: a statistical judge model that compares per-step gradient statistics against the verifier's own reference run, and a spot-check that re-executes randomly chosen training segments whose checkpoints are committed to the ledger one epoch at a time. On an MLP, a CNN, and an autoencoder at 100k-500k parameters, under gradient sign flipping, label poisoning, and fabricated runs, SciCheck reaches F1 of 0.966, 0.906, and 0.976 while rejecting 0-4.4% of honest runs. For the attacks we test, the spot-check accounts for most of this accuracy, and we characterize what each layer can detect. The effective-signal statistic falls as $(1-2s)^2$ under a sign flip of severity $s$, so gradient statistics alone catch strong enough sign flips, while fabricated runs that replay genuine statistics look no more anomalous than honest runs to any statistical detector we test. The spot-check catches fully fabricated segments with probability at least $1-(1-s)^k$ for $k$ sampled segments when the draws stay hidden until the run is committed, which roughly matches our measurements and lets a verifier choose $k$ for a target soundness. Verification costs 1.5-2.3x one retraining, mostly for the verifier's reference run; each submission's spot-check takes under 1.5 s, and per-epoch commitments reduce checkpoint storage 5-17x compared with keeping every checkpoint.

### 4. On the (non-)closedness of the set of ReLU networks

**中文标题**: On the (non-)closedness of the set of ReLU networks

**作者**: Eloi Martinet, Leon Bungert

**中文摘要**: We prove that the set $\mathcal{N}_N$ of ReLU networks with one hidden layer and at most $N\in\mathbb N$ neurons is weakly closed in $W^{1,p}(Ω)$, for a bounded connected open set $Ω\subset\mathbb R^d$. Consequently, every coercive, weakly lower semicontinuous energy on this space attains its minimum over $\mathcal{N}_N$. This covers the Deep Ritz method with convex integrands, Sobolev training and Lipschitz learning. Moreover, as the weight decay parameter tends to zero, minimizers of the regularized problem converge, up to subsequences, to minimizers with minimal parameter norm. We also prove that the set of ReLU networks with two hidden layers of bounded widths is not closed in $W^{1,p}(Ω)$ or in $C(\overlineΩ)$. This disproves a conjecture of Petersen, Raslan and Voigtlaender.

### 5. FlowMoP: Stochastic Multi-Person Motion Prediction

**中文标题**: FlowMoP: Stochastic Multi-Person Motion Prediction

**作者**: Aadya Agrawal, Ho Kei Cheng, Alexander Schwing

**中文摘要**: Predicting the future motion of multiple people is inherently stochastic: the same observed history admits many plausible continuations depending on intent, coordination, and social context. Yet the dominant paradigm in multi-person 3D motion prediction remains deterministic, with existing methods producing a fixed number of outputs per agent. In contrast, we present a conditional flow matching framework that jointly generates diverse future motions for multiple agents, modeling both global trajectories and root-relative poses. A B-spline reparameterization compresses the generative state space while enforcing temporal smoothness, and a dual-stream transformer conditions each agent's predictions on observed neighbor motion for interaction-aware forecasting. Our method achieves state-of-the-art performance on CMU-Mocap (UMPM) and 3DPW, outperforming deterministic baselines on joint position error, pose error, and final displacement error despite producing a full predictive distribution. We additionally establish the first motion prediction baseline on WorldPose, a large-scale professional soccer dataset, where social conditioning demonstrably concentrates predictive uncertainty along interaction-constrained directions.
